bridge_agents

Phase 20 (specs/multiagent/phase20/, experiment C1): the agents’ side of the bridge board. The rule sentences (COUPLE_RULE, MIRROR_RULE, BRIDGE_RULE, which open with the one exception to the unchanged squad rule) and bridgeRules; bridgeLines, at most three observation lines (a holder’s mirrors, a bridge’s pair, the squad’s coupled subtasks); parseBridge (mirror shares, which never reach the channel, and relays); the numpy members RuleBridge (relays, and honest mirror posts, P22-12) and MirrorLiar (scripted liars that also lie on mirrors), applied by bridgeMembers through colony_board.stage4Members; and bridgePromptTokens, the longest prompt per role in a rollout. Without a bridge key nothing changes.

Rules, lines and parsing

source

parseBridge

def parseBridge(
    env, parsed, i
):

TextClueEnv.parse’s bridge cases -> (action, report, valid), or None for parse’s own path. A share on one of i’s mirrors: valid at the cross clue’s true residue from a holder (an adaptive liar: any residue), with report None, so nothing reaches the channel; anything else there is invalid. A bridge’s share on its pair’s open coupled subtask of a value posted on the mirror (any value, P20-4): a relay, valid and posted as an ordinary share.

source

bridgeLines

def bridgeLines(
    view, i, t:NoneType=None
):

At most three observation lines for agent i on a bridge board ([] on any other): its mirrors, for holders of a cross clue (P20-16; liar slots: all), with the clue it holds and the values posted there; for a bridge, its pair’s mirror posts (P20-3); for every agent, its squad’s open subtasks that need the next squad.

source

bridgeRules

def bridgeRules(
    env, i
):

The bridge sentences for agent i, each after a blank line (’’ on any other board, or without i): COUPLE_RULE and MIRROR_RULE on a coupled board (every squad holds cross clues for the one before it, so any agent may hold one), BRIDGE_RULE for a bridge (a guarding bridge keeps GUARD_RULE before it). SQUAD_RULE is unchanged (P20-15).

Rule agents, liars and prompt length

source

bridgePromptTokens

def bridgePromptTokens(
    cfg, seeds, tok:NoneType=None, policy:NoneType=None
):

{‘bridge’, ‘holder’, ‘other’: n}: the longest rendered prompt per role over a rollout of cfg on seeds (scripted faults as runEval; policy default: the fake Claude client). Role per turn: bridge slots; holders, turns that show a mirror line; the rest. tok: a tokenizer (chat-template text, as LocalPolicy) or text -> count; default about 4 characters a token.

source

bridgeMembers

def bridgeMembers(
    world, members
):

A run’s numpy members on a bridge board (stage4Members’ hook, so every numpy path gets it): scripted ClueLiars become MirrorLiar; honest rule agents (not liars or mistaken members) gain RuleBridge (their class becomes Bridge). InSquad wrappers are kept (their inner member changes); Claude members are left alone. Returns members.

source

MirrorLiar

def MirrorLiar(
    channel, rng
):

ClueLiar that counts its squad’s mirrors (all of them, P20-5) among its targets: on a lying turn it draws k from open + mirrors with rng.integers as ClueLiar does, and on a mirror posts a false residue of clue 1 (to the board only). With no mirrors (no bridge key, or none open) its draws are ClueLiar’s.

source

RuleBridge

def RuleBridge(
    channel, rng, share:str='needed', guess:float=0.0
):

Mixin for an honest numpy member on a bridge board (bridgeMembers builds Bridge<class> from its class, so knownFor and everything else stay the base agent’s: a ColonyAgent on colony arms, flag-aware on failFlag boards). Roles are read at act time. A bridge (board.bridgeOf) first relays the leading mirror value (most senders, then earliest) of an open coupled subtask of its pair with no value of the cross clue on its board; otherwise it acts as its base agent. Every member, at its sharing step, first posts a held cross clue on its mirror when the mirror’s leading value is missing, different or unconfirmed (ColonyAgent’s rule, P22-12). A mirror share is applied to the board only, never posted to the channel.

source

onMirror

def onMirror(
    b, i, action
):

Whether action is a share on another squad’s coupled subtask, i.e. on one of agent i’s mirrors (never posted to the channel).

import numpy as np

def play(board, cls, slots=(0, 1, 2), T=30):
    "Liars at `slots`, acting alone in turn order on their squad's view; their actions."
    ms, out = [cls(ClueBoard(), np.random.default_rng(7 + i)) for i in slots], []
    for m, i in zip(ms, slots):
        m.id = i
    for t in range(1, T):
        for m in ms:
            v = squadView(board, m.id)
            v.advance(t)
            a = m.choose(v, t)
            board.apply(m.id, a, t)
            out.append(a)
    return out

def bridgeFake(req):
    "fakeReply, except that a bridge relays its pair's first mirror value and a holder shares its mirror clue on some turns."
    o = req['messages'][0]['content'][-1]['text']
    h = int(hashlib.sha256(o.encode()).hexdigest()[:8], 16)
    say = lambda k, c: json.dumps(dict(action='share', target=f'T{k}', answer=0, clue=c, notes=f'h{h % 7}'))
    if (m := re.search(r'^From squad \d+ for your subtasks: T(\d+): (x mod \d+ = \d+)', o, re.M)) and h % 2:
        return say(*m.groups())
    if (m := re.search(r'^For squad \d+ \(share your clue; you cannot answer\): .*?T(\d+): (x mod \d+ = \d+) \(yours\)', o, re.M)) and h % 3:
        return say(*m.groups())
    if 'secretly' in req['system'][0]['text'] and (m := re.search(r'^For squad .*?T(\d+): x mod (\d+)', o, re.M)) and h % 2:
        return say(m.group(1), f'x mod {m.group(2)} = {h % int(m.group(2))}')
    return fakeReply(req)
